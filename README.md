# news-image-20261117

One image summing up the world news of Tuesday 17 November 2026, built for make.fun's "Sum up 17 November's world news in one image" bounty.

The image is a 1024x1024 front page: a masthead naming the day, and six stories across regions and fields, each with a drawn icon, a short headline and a one-line summary. Every story happened, or was first reported, on 17 November 2026, as reported by Reuters, AP and PAHO:

1. **U.S. strikes Iran** — missile sites hit on Qeshm Island after tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz.
2. **Ukraine hits Kerch Bridge** — Kyiv calls it "unprecedented"; Russia strikes bridges in Kyiv in reply.
3. **Japan scrambles jets** — two suspected Russian aircraft enter Japanese airspace; Tokyo protests.
4. **Measles-free status lost** — PAHO: U.S. elimination status gone, first time since 2000.
5. **Tech outage wave** — airports, banks and Apple disrupted; NSA internal network goes offline.
6. **Trump in Manila** — arrives for the ASEAN summit; next: China for APEC and Xi–Putin talks.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` to the output directory, so both runs write byte-identical output. The image itself was drawn programmatically with PIL at authoring time (flat icons, no photographs, no copied artwork).
